## 🎨 Symbolism & Design Anatomy

The **SurvScribe** logo emblem unites four foundational loss assessment elements:
1. **The Shield / Forensic Lens Outline**: Represents policy protection, insurable interest, risk verification, and forensic objectivity.
2. **The Structured Survey Document**: Represents the standardized 9-section report, itemized damage registers, and mathematical quantification sheets.
3. **The Scribe Nib (Gold & Cobalt)**: Symbolizes the professional surveyor's pen rapidly drafting factual findings and legally sound opinions.
4. **The AI Intelligence Spark (4-Point Star)**: Symbolizes grounded, zero-hallucination AI acceleration turning raw field evidence into completed reports in minutes.
5. **The Verified Green Seal**: Represents verified arithmetic, matched invoices, and regulatory compliance.
